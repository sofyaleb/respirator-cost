Assumptions

Change any value and the estimate updates. The range uses the low and high values. Changing a value moves its range with it.

people covered = population × group share
disposables = people × shifts per month × months × masks per shift
reusables = people × spares, plus filters each month
total = respirators + filters + fit testing & training + storage & distribution
